# US Edition usage guide (one page)

Published by Glint. **General information only, not legal advice.**

This US Edition sits alongside the France/EU materials in your pack. All amounts below are in **USD**.

---

## Recommended order

1. **`us-written-contract-checklist.md`**  
   Before new US client work (or when total fees may cross $250 / $500 / $800 thresholds), confirm whether a written contract may be required in New York State, New York City, Illinois, or California. Use official links in that file.

2. **`us-unpaid-invoice-tracker.csv`**  
   Import into Google Sheets, Excel, or Notion. One row per invoice. Fill `state_or_city` and `law_possibly_applicable` when relevant. Sort by `days_overdue` descending.

3. **`us-chase-emails.md`**  
   Copy the email that matches your timeline: before due date, due date, D+7, D+14, D+30, then final email before escalation. Use the short text/Slack block only where you already message the client. Use the excuse replies when clients stall.

4. **`us-final-demand-letter.md`**  
   After the D+30 sequence, send a formal final demand (certified mail or tracked email). Fill agency names only if you verified them on the official site.

5. **EU/FR tools still in the pack**  
   For French e-invoicing or EU interest, keep using `e-invoicing-*` and `payment-reminder-emails-en.md` as needed. The US files do not replace tax or VAT rules outside the US.

## Timeline at a glance

| Stage | Suggested action | File |
|-------|------------------|------|
| Upcoming due date | Friendly heads-up | `us-chase-emails.md` §1 |
| Due date | Same-day nudge | §2 |
| 7 days late | First overdue email | §3 |
| 14 days late | Firm email + optional law line | §4 |
| 30 days late | Escalation warning | §5 |
| Before court/agency | Final email + letter | §6 + `us-final-demand-letter.md` |

Update the tracker after every touch (`last_contact_date`, `next_action`).
