# US chase email templates: Freelancer Overdue Invoice Kit (US Edition)

Personal use. Replace [FIRST_NAME], [CLIENT_COMPANY], [INVOICE], [AMOUNT_USD], [DUE_DATE], [PAYMENT_LINK], [YOUR_NAME], [YOUR_BUSINESS], [DATE], [DATE_PLUS_7], [DATE_PLUS_14], [PAYMENT_DEADLINE], [STATE_OR_CITY], [LAW_NAME], [AGENCY_NAME], [AGENCY_URL].

**Templates for general information only, not legal advice.** Late fees, interest, and recovery steps apply only where your contract and applicable law allow. If a state or city freelance payment law may apply, use a neutral optional line such as: "If the [LAW_NAME] applies to our contract, please note that timely payment may be required under that law (see [AGENCY_URL])."

Published by Glint.

## Short text / Slack-style follow-ups

Use only on channels where you already work with the client. One message at a time. No threats.

**Soft (D+3 to D+7)**  
Hi [FIRST_NAME], quick ping on invoice [INVOICE] ($[AMOUNT_USD], due [DUE_DATE]). Can you confirm AP has it? Thanks, [YOUR_NAME]

**Firm (D+14)**  
[FIRST_NAME], [INVOICE] is 14 days past due ($[AMOUNT_USD]). Please confirm payment this week or your billing contact. [YOUR_NAME]

**Urgent (before final letter)**  
[FIRST_NAME], final check-in on [INVOICE] ($[AMOUNT_USD]). I need payment or a plan by [PAYMENT_DEADLINE] or I will send a formal demand. [YOUR_NAME]

**Slack thread reply**  
Thanks for the update. Please loop in AP on [INVOICE] ($[AMOUNT_USD], due [DUE_DATE]). Payment link: [PAYMENT_LINK]

## Replies to common client excuses

**"We never received the invoice."**  
Thanks for letting me know. I have re-attached invoice [INVOICE] ($[AMOUNT_USD] USD, due [DUE_DATE]). Please confirm receipt and the expected payment date.

**"It is with accounting / AP."**  
Understood. Could you share the AP ticket number or contact email? I will send a copy directly and note [INVOICE] for $[AMOUNT_USD] due [DUE_DATE].

**"We need a PO first."**  
Happy to help. Please send the PO number or the person who can issue it. Once received, I will update invoice [INVOICE] and confirm the payment date for $[AMOUNT_USD].

**"Cash flow is tight. Can we pay later?"**  
I can consider a short written plan. Example: 50% by [DATE_PLUS_7] and balance by [DATE_PLUS_14]. Please confirm in writing so we can close [INVOICE] ($[AMOUNT_USD]).

**"We are disputing the work."**  
Please specify what is disputed in writing (scope, hours, deliverables). I will respond within [X] business days. Any undisputed portion of invoice [INVOICE] should still be paid per our agreement.

**"Net 60 / Net 90 is our policy."**  
Our agreement and invoice [INVOICE] show payment due [DUE_DATE]. If your policy differs, please send the contract section or approved exception so we can align on $[AMOUNT_USD].

**"Check is in the mail."**  
Thank you. Please share the check number, amount, and mail date. If it does not arrive by [DATE_PLUS_7], I will follow up again on [INVOICE].
