# Payment reminder email templates: Overdue Invoice Kit

Personal use. Replace [FIRST_NAME], [INVOICE], [AMOUNT], [CURRENCY], [DUE_DATE], [PAYMENT_LINK], [YOUR_NAME], [DATE], [DATE+5], [YOUR_TAX_ID] if relevant.

**Templates for information only, not legal advice.** Late payment interest and recovery costs apply only where your contract and local law allow.

**France e-invoicing (2026):** for platform-specific statuses and templates 16 to 19, use `e-invoicing-chase-emails-en.md` and the e-invoicing checklists in this pack.

---

## 1. D+7: Gentle reminder
**Subject:** Quick follow-up: invoice [INVOICE]

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

A quick note about invoice [INVOICE] for [AMOUNT] [CURRENCY], due [DUE_DATE].

Can you confirm when payment is scheduled?

Payment link: [PAYMENT_LINK]

Thanks,
[YOUR_NAME]

[YOUR_NAME]

## 9. D+30: Formal demand (email)

[YOUR_NAME]
