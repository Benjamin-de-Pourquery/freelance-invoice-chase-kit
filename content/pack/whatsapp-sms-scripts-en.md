# WhatsApp / SMS scripts: Overdue Invoice Kit

Personal use. Replace [FIRST_NAME], [INVOICE], [AMOUNT], [CURRENCY], [DUE_DATE], [PAYMENT_LINK], [DATE], [YOUR_NAME].

**This is not legal advice.**

---

## Usage notes

- **WhatsApp**: use when you already communicate with the client on this channel. Stay professional. Avoid threats or wording that could be unlawful (harassment, defamation, false claims).
- **SMS**: good for a short nudge when email goes unanswered. One clear message. Do not spam.
- Send after at least one documented email reminder (see `payment-reminder-emails-en.md`).
- Only mention formal demand letters or legal steps you actually intend to take.

---

## Soft level

1. Hi [FIRST_NAME], quick reminder on invoice [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]), due [DUE_DATE]. Can you confirm a payment date? Thanks, [YOUR_NAME]

2. Hey [FIRST_NAME], I sent an email about [INVOICE]. Is everything ok on your accounting side? Link if needed: [PAYMENT_LINK]

3. Hi [FIRST_NAME], invoice [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]) is still open on your end. Let me know if you need any details. [YOUR_NAME]

4. Hello [FIRST_NAME], short note: [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]). When do you expect to pay? Thanks.

5. Hi [FIRST_NAME], did you receive invoice [INVOICE]? Due [DUE_DATE]. Happy to help. [YOUR_NAME]

---

## Firm level

1. Hi [FIRST_NAME], invoice [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]) has been outstanding since [DUE_DATE]. Please confirm a transfer this week: [PAYMENT_LINK]

2. [FIRST_NAME], without an update on [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]), I will need to pause upcoming deliverables until payment. Can you confirm a date?

3. Hi [FIRST_NAME], follow-up on [INVOICE]. Amount [AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]. Please settle by [DATE] or propose a firm payment plan.

4. [FIRST_NAME], I have emailed several times about [INVOICE]. Can you forward to accounts payable or share the billing contact? [YOUR_NAME]

5. Hi [FIRST_NAME], invoice [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]): I need a payment date within 5 business days. Link: [PAYMENT_LINK]

---

## Urgent level

1. Hi [FIRST_NAME], without payment for [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]) by [DATE], I will move to a formal written follow-up (formal demand per my process). Please prioritize: [PAYMENT_LINK]

2. [FIRST_NAME], final friendly reminder on [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]). Without a reply within 48 hours, I will escalate with a formal email or letter.

3. Hi [FIRST_NAME], [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]) is overdue since [DUE_DATE]. I am pausing access / deliverables until payment. Please confirm transfer: [PAYMENT_LINK]

4. [FIRST_NAME], you mentioned payment on [DATE] for [INVOICE]. Can you confirm today? Otherwise I need to escalate internally.

5. Hi [FIRST_NAME], action required on [INVOICE] ([AMOUNT] [CURRENCY]). Please pay by [DATE]. Late payment interest and recovery costs may apply if allowed by your contract and local law.
