# UK chase email templates: Freelancer Overdue Invoice Kit (UK Edition)

Personal use. Replace [CLIENT_CONTACT], [CLIENT_COMPANY], [INVOICE], [AMOUNT_GBP], [DUE_DATE], [PAYMENT_LINK], [YOUR_BUSINESS], [DATE], [PAYMENT_DEADLINE], [REFERENCE_RATE_PERCENT], [STATUTORY_RATE_PERCENT].

**B2B commercial contracts only.** These templates are for general information only, not legal advice. Statutory late payment interest and fixed compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 and the Late Payment of Commercial Debts Regulations 2013 apply only where the statutory regime applies (for example, business-to-business contracts without a substantial contractual remedy for late payment). Where they may apply, you may be entitled to claim statutory interest (reference rate plus 8% per year) and a fixed compensation sum (40, 70, or 100 GBP depending on the debt size). See GOV.UK and legislation.gov.uk links in `uk-edition-guide.md`.

Published by Glint.

## 1. Friendly reminder (after due date)
**Subject:** Reminder: invoice [INVOICE] now overdue

Dear [CLIENT_CONTACT],

I hope you are well. Invoice [INVOICE] for £[AMOUNT_GBP] GBP was due on [DUE_DATE] and remains outstanding.

If anything is missing on your side (purchase order, remittance details, or accounts payable contact), please tell me and I will send it today.

Payment link or bank details: [PAYMENT_LINK]

Thank you for confirming when payment will be sent.

Kind regards,
[YOUR_BUSINESS]

---

## 2. Firm reminder
**Subject:** Action needed: invoice [INVOICE] ([DUE_DATE])

Dear [CLIENT_CONTACT],

Invoice [INVOICE] for £[AMOUNT_GBP] GBP is still unpaid after my earlier reminder.

Please pay in full by [PAYMENT_DEADLINE] or reply with a firm payment date and your accounts payable reference.

If our contract is a business-to-business agreement and the statutory late payment regime applies, please note that you may be required to pay statutory interest (Bank of England reference rate plus 8% per year, currently [REFERENCE_RATE_PERCENT]% + 8% = [STATUTORY_RATE_PERCENT]% per year if the reference rate is unchanged) and a fixed compensation sum for recovery costs (40, 70, or 100 GBP per invoice, depending on the amount owed). I may be entitled to claim these sums where the law allows. Official summary: https://www.gov.uk/late-commercial-payments-interest-debt-recovery/charging-interest-commercial-debt

[PAYMENT_LINK]

Regards,
[YOUR_BUSINESS]

---

## 3. Final reminder before letter before action
**Subject:** Final reminder: invoice [INVOICE] before formal letter

Dear [CLIENT_CONTACT],

This is my final email request for payment of £[AMOUNT_GBP] GBP for invoice [INVOICE], originally due [DUE_DATE].

Please pay in full by [PAYMENT_DEADLINE] using [PAYMENT_LINK] or the bank details on the invoice.

If I do not receive payment or a written payment plan by that date, I intend to send a **letter before action** and may pursue remedies permitted by our contract and applicable law, which can include statutory interest and fixed compensation where the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 applies, and court action in England and Wales via Money Claim Online where appropriate.

I would prefer to resolve this without further formal steps.

Sincerely,
[YOUR_BUSINESS]

## Short text follow-ups (optional)

Use only on channels where you already work with the client. One message at a time.

**Soft**  
Hi [CLIENT_CONTACT], quick reminder on invoice [INVOICE] (£[AMOUNT_GBP], due [DUE_DATE]). Can you confirm when accounts payable will pay? Thanks, [YOUR_BUSINESS]

**Firm**  
[CLIENT_CONTACT], invoice [INVOICE] is still open (£[AMOUNT_GBP]). Please confirm payment by [PAYMENT_DEADLINE] or your AP contact. [YOUR_BUSINESS]

**Before letter**  
[CLIENT_CONTACT], final check-in on [INVOICE] (£[AMOUNT_GBP]). I need payment or a plan by [PAYMENT_DEADLINE] or I will send a letter before action. [YOUR_BUSINESS]
